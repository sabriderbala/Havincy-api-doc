---
title: "Connect Claude"
description: "Add Havincy to claude.ai and Claude Desktop."
icon: "message"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** and click **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the server URL">
    Name it **Havincy** and paste:

    ```text
    https://havincy.com/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to Havincy, choose your workspace and permissions, and allow access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Enable the connector in a conversation and ask Claude, for example: *“List my brand kits, then create 3 Instagram visuals for the summer collection.”*
  </Step>
</Steps>

On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner may need to add the connector for the organization first.
