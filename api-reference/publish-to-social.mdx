---
title: "Publish to social networks"
api: "POST /social/publications"
---

Publishes ready media to the connected social accounts, now or at `scheduled_at`. One video, or up to 10 images as a carousel. Requires a plan with Diffusion.

Required permission: `publish`. MCP tool: `publish_to_social`.

<ParamField body="asset_ids" type="integer[]" required>
  Ready image or video asset ids (1 to 10).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="caption" type="string" required>
  Post caption, hashtags included (max 2200 characters).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="social_account_ids" type="integer[]">
  Target accounts from [List social accounts](/api-reference/list-social-accounts).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="platforms" type="string[]">
  Or target platforms (first connected account of each), e.g. `youtube`, `instagram`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="scheduled_at" type="string">
  ISO 8601 date-time in the future. Omit to publish now.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="options" type="object">
  Platform options.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="youtube_format" type="string">`short` or `video`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="title" type="string">Title (YouTube), max 100 characters.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>
